[size=6][b]Lazy Git[/b][/size]

Your base looks after itself. Fires, smelters, cooking stations, fermenters, ballistas, beehives and sap extractors are kept stocked from your chests, doors close behind you, damage is repaired, trees grow back and your shield is on when you draw a sword, so you never have to walk round with a stack of wood again.
Everything tedious in a base is left to a chest. Each chore has its own switch. The chest chores work only on what you built, using chests you are allowed to use.

[size=5][b]Features[/b][/size]

### Auto Fuel
[list]
[*]Campfires, hearths, bonfires, torches, sconces, braziers, the hot tub and other fires that burn fuel take [b]their own fuel[/b] (wood, resin, ...) from your chests.
[*]Chests up to [b]30 m[/b] away are used (a setting), the nearest first.
[*]A fire is filled up [b]in one go[/b] once its fuel is below half (a setting), so you hear it once, not all the time.
[*]A [b]switch for each kind of fire[/b], listed in the settings once you are in a world.
[*]Only the fires and chests [b]you built[/b] are used: the world's own fires (villages, dungeons) are left alone, as are carts, ships, gravestones, other players' private chests and guild chests, and any chest that is open at the time.
[*]Optionally uses the fuel you are carrying when the chests run out.
[*]Works in multiplayer. It is [b]required on the server and on every player[/b]: a player without it, or with a different version (1.0 against 1.1), cannot join. The chores are done by the players' games; the server's copy does nothing by itself.
[*]Translated into 9 languages.
[/list]
### Auto Stations
[list]
[*]Smelters, blast furnaces, charcoal kilns, spinning wheels, windmills and eitr refineries are [b]kept stocked[/b] from your chests: their fuel (charcoal, ...) and their raw material (ore, wood, flax, barley, ...) up to the most they can hold.
[*]Chests up to [b]30 m[/b] away are used (a setting), the nearest first.
[*]A [b]switch for each kind of station[/b], listed in the settings once you are in a world.
[*]Same rules as Auto Fuel: only what [b]you built[/b], only your chests, and it works in multiplayer.
[/list]
### Auto Cooking
[list]
[*]Cooking stations, iron cooking stations and grills are [b]loaded with raw food[/b] from your chests while their fire is lit.
[*]Where they burn fuel, it is topped up from the chests too.
[*]Food that is [b]done is taken off[/b] before it can burn and put [b]into a chest[/b] (one that already holds some first, then the nearest). If no chest has room, it [b]stays on the station[/b] until there is: nothing is dropped on the floor.
[*]Ovens are looked after by Auto Stations.
[*]A [b]switch for each kind of cooking station[/b], and the same rules as the other chores: only what you built, only your chests.
[/list]
### Auto Fermenting
[list]
[*]Fermentation barrels are [b]filled with mead base[/b] from your chests as soon as they are empty.
[*]When a barrel is [b]ready it is tapped[/b], and the mead goes [b]into a chest[/b] (one that already holds some first, then the nearest). If the chests have no room for all of it, the barrel [b]waits[/b] until they do: nothing is dropped on the floor.
[*]A [b]switch for each kind of fermenter[/b], and the same rules as the other chores.
[/list]
### Auto Ballista
[list]
[*]Ballistas you built are [b]restocked with bolts[/b] from your chests, so the base defends itself without a trip round the walls.
[*]A ballista that has some bolts keeps its kind of bolt; an empty one takes whichever kind the chests have.
[*]Filled up in one go once it is below half (a setting), from chests up to [b]30 m[/b] away.
[*]A [b]switch for each kind of ballista[/b], and the same rules as the other chores.
[/list]
### Auto Shield Generators
[list]
[*]The [b]shield generators[/b] you built are topped up with their fuel ([b]black cores[/b]) from your chests, so the dome stays up.
[*]Filled up in one go once the fuel is below half (a setting), from chests up to [b]30 m[/b] away. A [b]switch for each kind of generator[/b], and the same rules as the other chores.
[/list]
### Auto Open Doors
[list]
[*]Doors and gates you built [b]open when you walk up to them[/b], away from you, within [b]3 m[/b] of their nearest part (a setting).
[*]A door you [b]close by hand stays closed[/b] until you have walked away from it. Doors that need a key, and doors that cannot be closed, are left alone, as are doors in a ward you have no access to.
[*][b]Double doors[/b] (two doors of the same kind side by side) act as one: both open together, and both are closed only once nobody is near either half. The gap that counts is a setting.
[*]Which kinds of door are covered is set in [b]Door Types[/b], shared with Auto Close Doors. It only works for you: each player opens doors for themselves.
[/list]
### Auto Close Doors
[list]
[*]Doors and gates you built that are [b]left open[/b] are closed again once [b]nobody has been near them[/b] for 10 seconds (both settings).
[*]Doors that need a key, and doors that cannot be closed, are left alone.
[*]A [b]switch for each kind of door[/b]. In multiplayer a door is closed by the player who owns it in the game's network, so two players never fight over one.
[/list]
### Auto Repair Buildings
[list]
[*]Worn and damaged pieces [b]you built[/b], within [b]40 m[/b] of you, are repaired once their health is below [b]75%[/b] (both settings), the way the hammer repairs them, so it costs no materials.
[*]Not in a ward you have no access to, and a badly damaged base is mended over a few rounds rather than all at once.
[/list]
### Auto Repair Gear
[list]
[*][b]Open a crafting station and your gear is repaired[/b]: no clicking the repair button again and again. Works at the workbench, forge, black forge, galdr table and other stations (including modded ones).
[*]Repairs the equipment in your [b]inventory[/b], worn or not. The game's own rules still apply: a workbench does not repair forge gear, undamaged items are left alone and repair costs are unchanged.
[/list]
### Auto Shield
[list]
[*][b]Drawing a one-handed weapon equips a shield with it[/b], including after a bow or crossbow. Two-handed and ranged weapons put the shield away.
[*]Choose which shield: the [b]first[/b] in your inventory, the [b]best[/b] blocker, or a [b]named[/b] one. Shields can be put on a never-use list, and the shield can be limited to certain weapons.
[*][b]No shield with axes[/b] (so you can chop trees) [b]or the butcher knife[/b] (for skinning), unless you want one.
[*]Sheathing with R keeps both weapon and shield on your back, or puts the shield away too (a setting). Putting the weapon away with its hotbar key puts the shield away; throwing a spear does not.
[/list]
### Auto Replant
[list]
[*]A [b]sapling is planted[/b] where a tree stump is destroyed, so the forest grows back. No seeds needed.
[*]Replant [b]chance[/b], a [b]delay[/b], an option for a [b]random sapling[/b], and [b]no planting near workbenches[/b] are settings.
[*][b]Stumps disappear by themselves[/b] as soon as a tree is cut down (a setting).
[*]Beech, birch, oak, fir, pine, ancient (swamp), yggdrasil, ashwood and snow fir trees are replanted. Only the player who owns the stump plants, so in multiplayer one sapling is planted, not one for each player.
[/list]
### Auto Collect
[list]
[*][b]Beehives[/b] and [b]sap extractors[/b] you built are emptied [b]into a chest[/b] within [b]30 m[/b] (a setting): one that already holds some honey or sap first, then the nearest.
[*]Emptied when full (a setting), so nothing is wasted. If no chest has room, it stays in the hive or extractor.
[*]Honey and sap can each be switched off.
[/list]
### Auto Egg Pickup
[list]
[*][b]Chicken and Asksvin eggs are picked up[/b] when you walk close to them, like other items on the ground when the game's auto pickup is on.
[*]Optionally [b]leave warm eggs[/b] (the ones that are hatching) where they are. A new egg is picked up only once it has been seen to be cold, a few seconds after it appears.
[*]Eggs you drop yourself are not picked up again at once.
[/list]
### Auto Farming
[list]
[*]Crops that have [b]grown[/b] are [b]harvested into a chest[/b] within [b]30 m[/b] (a setting): one that already holds some first, then the nearest. The harvest is what a click gives, including the chance of more with farming skill. If no chest has room, the crop stays on the field until there is.
[*]The [b]same plant is planted again[/b] where the crop stood, with the [b]seeds from your chests[/b] (a setting). With no seeds the spot is left empty.
[*]A [b]switch for each crop[/b]. Wild bushes and mushrooms are left alone, and so are crops that give extra items besides their harvest.
[/list]
### Auto Light Fires
[list]
[*]Fires you built that the game lets you [b]switch on and off[/b] are [b]lit when it gets dark[/b] and [b]put out in daylight[/b] (a setting), which saves their fuel.
[*]A fire is switched once at each change from day to night and back, so one you switch by hand in between stays as you left it. A fire with no fuel is not lit, and a wet one is left to the game.
[*]A [b]switch for each kind of fire[/b].
[/list]
### Auto Tidy
[list]
[*][b]Stackable items lying on the ground[/b] beside your [b]smelters, kilns, cooking stations and fermenters[/b] (what they turn out) are [b]put in a chest[/b]. A stack goes in whole, or stays where it is.
[*]Items you dropped yourself, eggs being left to hatch, and anything that is not stackable (weapons, armour) are left alone. How close to a station, how long to wait and how far the chests may be are settings.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Settings[/b][/size]

Press [b]F7[/b] in game to open the settings panel, or edit [b]BepInEx/config/MagicMike.LazyGit.cfg[/b].
[list]
[*][b]Enable Plugin[/b] (default On): Turns the mod on or off. Needs a restart.
[*][b]Enable Auto Fuel[/b] (default On): Fires take their fuel from your chests.
[*][b]Chest Range[/b] (default 30): Fires take fuel from chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Refill Below[/b] (default 50): A fire is filled up once its fuel is below this percentage. At 100 it is topped up whenever there is room for a piece of fuel.
[*][b]Use Player Inventory[/b] (default Off): Uses fuel you are carrying when the chests do not have enough.
[*][b]Show Message[/b] (default Off): Shows how much fuel the fires took, in the top-left corner.
[*][b]Fire Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of fire.
[*][b]Enable Auto Stations[/b] (default On): Stations are kept stocked from your chests.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Stations)[/b] (default 30): Stations take from chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Station Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of station.
[*][b]Enable Auto Cooking[/b] (default On): Cooking stations are loaded and emptied automatically.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Cooking)[/b] (default 30): Cooking stations take from chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Load Raw Food[/b] (default On): Raw food from the chests is put on the station while its fire is lit.
[*][b]Take Off Cooked Food[/b] (default On): Done food is taken off before it burns.
[*][b]Put Cooked Food In Chests[/b] (default On): Taken-off food goes into a chest in range; if none has room it stays on the station. If Off, it is dropped beside the station.
[*][b]Cooking Station Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of cooking station.
[*][b]Enable Auto Fermenting[/b] (default On): Fermenters are filled and tapped automatically.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Fermenting)[/b] (default 30): Fermenters use chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Fill Empty Fermenters[/b] (default On): An empty fermenter takes mead base from the chests.
[*][b]Tap Ready Fermenters[/b] (default On): A ready fermenter is tapped.
[*][b]Put Drinks In Chests[/b] (default On): Tapped drinks go into a chest in range; if there is no room for all of them the barrel waits. If Off, they come out of the tap.
[*][b]Fermenter Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of fermenter.
[*][b]Enable Auto Ballista[/b] (default On): Ballistas are restocked from your chests.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Ballista)[/b] (default 30): Ballistas take from chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Refill Below (Auto Ballista)[/b] (default 50): A ballista is filled up once its ammunition is below this percentage.
[*][b]Ballista Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of ballista.
[*][b]Enable Auto Shield Generators[/b] (default On): Shield generators are topped up with fuel from your chests.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Shield Generators)[/b] (default 30): Shield generators take fuel from chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Refill Below (Auto Shield Generators)[/b] (default 50): A generator is filled up once its fuel is below this percentage.
[*][b]Shield Generator Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of shield generator.
[*][b]Enable Auto Open Doors[/b] (default On): Doors and gates you built open when you walk up to them.
[*][b]Open Distance[/b] (default 3): Metres from the nearest part of a door at which it opens (never more than Player Distance).
[*][b]Pair Double Doors[/b] (default On): Two doors of the same kind side by side open and close together.
[*][b]Pair Distance[/b] (default 2.5): The largest gap, in metres, between two doors that still count as a double door.
[*][b]Enable Auto Close Doors[/b] (default On): Open doors with nobody near them are closed.
[*][b]Close After[/b] (default 10): Seconds an open door has had nobody near it before it is closed.
[*][b]Player Distance[/b] (default 6): A door stays open while a player is within this many metres.
[*][b]Door Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of door.
[*][b]Enable Auto Collect[/b] (default On): Beehives and sap extractors are emptied into your chests.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Collect)[/b] (default 30): The honey and sap go into chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Collect At[/b] (default 100): A hive or extractor is emptied once it is this percentage full.
[*][b]Collect Honey[/b] (default On): Beehives are emptied.
[*][b]Collect Sap[/b] (default On): Sap extractors are emptied.
[*][b]Enable Auto Repair Buildings[/b] (default On): Damaged pieces near you are repaired.
[*][b]Range (Auto Repair Buildings)[/b] (default 40): Pieces up to this many metres from you are repaired.
[*][b]Repair Below (Auto Repair Buildings)[/b] (default 75): A piece is repaired once its health is below this percentage.
[*][b]Enable Auto Repair Gear[/b] (default On): Opening a crafting station repairs your gear that it can repair.
[*][b]Enable Auto Shield[/b] (default On): A shield is equipped with a one-handed weapon.
[*][b]Equip With One-Handed Weapons[/b] (default On): A shield is equipped whenever a one-handed weapon is.
[*][b]Equip After Bow Or Crossbow[/b] (default On): Also equips a shield when you change from a bow or other two-handed weapon to a one-handed one.
[*][b]Put Away With Two-Handed Weapons[/b] (default On): The shield is put away when a two-handed or ranged weapon is equipped.
[*][b]Put Away When Sheathing[/b] (default Off): Sheathing (R) also puts the shield away.
[*][b]Put Away When Weapon Is Deselected[/b] (default On): The shield is put away when you put the weapon away with its hotbar key.
[*][b]Equip With Axes[/b] (default Off): A one-handed axe gets a shield.
[*][b]Equip With Butcher Knife[/b] (default Off): The butcher knife gets a shield.
[*][b]Shield Choice[/b] (default First): First in the inventory, Best (blocks most) or Preferred (the one named below).
[*][b]Preferred Shield[/b] (default ): The prefab name of the shield used when the choice is Preferred, for example [b]ShieldWood[/b].
[*][b]Weapons[/b] (default ): Only these one-handed weapons get a shield (types, skills or prefab names, separated by commas). Empty: all of them.
[*][b]Never Use These Shields[/b] (default ): Shields never equipped automatically (prefab names, separated by commas).
[*][b]Enable Auto Replant[/b] (default On): A sapling is planted where a stump is destroyed.
[*][b]Replant Chance[/b] (default 100): The chance, in percent, that a tree is replanted.
[*][b]Delay[/b] (default 2.5): Seconds before the sapling is planted.
[*][b]Random Sapling[/b] (default Off): A sapling of a random kind is planted.
[*][b]Remove Stumps[/b] (default On): Stumps disappear as soon as a tree is cut down.
[*][b]Keep Clear Of Workbenches[/b] (default 0): No sapling within this many metres of a workbench. 0 is off.
[*][b]Replant Swamp Trees[/b] (default On): Ancient (swamp) trees are replanted too.
[*][b]Ignore Growth Space[/b] (default Off): Saplings grow even when there is not enough room around them.
[*][b]Enable Auto Egg Pickup[/b] (default On): Chicken and Asksvin eggs are picked up when you walk close to them.
[*][b]Leave Warm Eggs[/b] (default Off): Hatching (warm) eggs are left where they are.
[*][b]Enable Auto Farming[/b] (default On): Grown crops are harvested into your chests and planted again.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Farming)[/b] (default 30): Crops go into chests, and seeds come from them, up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Plant Again[/b] (default On): The same plant is planted again with the seeds from the chests.
[*][b]Crop Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each crop.
[*][b]Enable Auto Light Fires[/b] (default On): Fires you built that can be switched on and off are lit when it gets dark.
[*][b]Put Out In Daylight[/b] (default On): The fires are put out when the day comes.
[*][b]Light Types[/b] (default On): One switch for each kind of fire.
[*][b]Enable Auto Tidy[/b] (default On): Stackable items beside your stations are put in a chest.
[*][b]Chest Range (Auto Tidy)[/b] (default 30): The items go into chests up to this many metres away.
[*][b]Station Distance[/b] (default 4): Only items within this many metres of one of your stations are tidied.
[*][b]Wait[/b] (default 5): Seconds an item lies on the ground before it is tidied.
[/list]

[size=5][b]Translations[/b][/size]

The translation files are in [b]BepInEx/config/LazyGit/Translations/<Language>/LazyGit.json[/b]. Edit them, or add a language, and restart the game.

[size=5][b]Installation[/b][/size]

### Automatic Install
Plugin is created to be installed with r2modman or Gale.
For Nexus downloads you will need to install manually.
### Manual Install
[list]
[*]Un-zip and drag the [b]LazyGit[/b] folder in your [b]BepInEx/plugins/[/b] folder.
[/list]
